Our mission

Clean energy and climate resilience solutions already exist and new innovations are emerging, but they aren't being deployed fast enough.

We connect communities with technologies and invest catalytic capital so practical solutions get adopted and deliver real impact, faster.

CruxLine

In outdoor adventures, the crux is the hardest part of any route.

On expeditions, a new line is a path successfully completed for the first time.

CruxLine invests the catalytic capital needed to get practical solutions over the crux of deployment, pioneering new models for future success.

Our why

We all have a connection to the outdoors.

Whether it's on epic adventures, out doing sport, running local trails, walking the dog, or sharing a sunset with friends, the outdoors brings us together. The benefits, inspiration and common ground it creates matter now more than ever.

But spending time outside, also means we experience first-hand the accelerating impacts of a changing climate on the places we love.

The outdoors inspires action. And action isn't optional for the wild places, or the people who depend on them.

How we work

Investing to accelerate solutions

Identify

Working with communities, local organisations and outdoor institutions to identify opportunities that increase energy affordability and reliability, climate resilience and healthy natural environments.

Develop

Providing technical, commercial and financial expertise while bringing together the partners needed to deliver each project.

Catalyse

Designing and deploying the most appropriate form of catalytic capital - matching the right financial instrument, to the right project, at the right stage.

Deploy

Mobilising follow-on commercial investment so projects become financially sustainable and capable of long-term growth.

Scale

Capital is invested, recovered and recycled into future projects, perpetually generating multiple rounds of impact.
